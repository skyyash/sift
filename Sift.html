Gestures

Tap
Next sentence, at your own pace.
Slide
Drag left or right to seek through the article, like scrubbing a video. The progress line at the top works the same way.
Scroll up
Only the text you have already read scrolls. Your current sentence stays put.
Double-tap
Highlight the sentence you are on.
Hold
Keep pressing, slide to Drop or Ask, then let go.

On a keyboard: Right arrow or Space is next, Left arrow is back, H highlights, D drops, A asks.
